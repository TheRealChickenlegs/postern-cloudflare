## Playing

### How do I move?

- **Tap an adjacent tile** to step onto it, or to attack whatever is standing
  there.
- **Tap a distant tile** to travel there automatically. Travel stops by itself
  when a monster comes into view, when you reach something interesting, or when
  the route forks.
- **Drag** in a direction to keep moving that way.
- **Press and hold** anywhere on the map for directional commands — Open, Bash,
  Tunnel, Disarm, Look and Jam. Pick one, then tap a direction.

### Why is there a keyboard hint under every button?

Because every command has a key, and an external keyboard is the fastest way to
play if you know the original. Connect one and the numeric keypad moves you:
`1`–`9` for the eight directions, `5` to wait. `o` opens, `s` searches, `q`
quaffs, `m` casts, `i` shows your inventory.

### What are the colours on the map?

The lit area is what your torch reaches. The dimmer area is what you have
explored and remember. Black is somewhere you have never been. Monsters are only
drawn where you can currently see them.

### I am stuck and cannot find the stairs

Search — the `Search` button, or `s` — reveals secret doors and traps in the
eight tiles around you. Secret doors look exactly like walls until found.

Watch your torch. When `LIGHT` reaches zero the map goes dark, and you cannot
search while blind. Eat before you get hungry: `Hungry` becomes `Weak`, and weak
characters start fainting at the worst possible moment.

### Can I undo a mistake?

No. Death is permanent, as it is in the tradition this game comes from. Your
run is recorded in the Hall of Records and that character is gone.

## Purchases

### What does the unlock do?

One purchase, once. It removes all advertising **and** lifts the limit on how
deep you can descend. Nothing else is locked — the whole game is there either
way, and the free version is a complete game up to the depth limit.

### How deep can I go for free?

You can descend to dungeon level 10. When you reach the staircase to 11 you will
be asked to unlock. **You are never trapped**: you can always walk back up, and
your character is untouched. Nothing is lost by declining.

### I bought the unlock but it is not showing

Open **Settings** inside the app and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are
signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

If it still does not appear, email us with your Apple Account email (we do not
need, and will not ask for, your password or payment details).

### Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit
<https://reportaproblem.apple.com> — we cannot issue refunds directly.

### Is the unlock shared with my family?

It follows Apple's standard rules for non-consumable purchases. If you have
Family Sharing enabled and the purchase is eligible, family members can use it.

## Saves and data

### Where are my saves?

On your device. Underdeep has no accounts and no servers — your save files never
leave your phone or iPad.

### I lost my save after reinstalling

Because saves are stored locally, deleting the app deletes them. They are
included in a normal iCloud or Finder device backup, so restoring your device
from a backup restores your saves. There is no cloud save of our own.

### Can I share a dungeon?

Yes. The title screen shows a **seed** — a number that reproduces your dungeon
exactly. Give someone the seed and they can play the same fifty floors.

## Accessibility

- **VoiceOver** labels every control, the character sheet, the message log and
  the action bar.
- **Dynamic Type** is honoured throughout the interface.
- **An external keyboard** provides a complete alternative to touch gestures.
- **Reduce Motion** is respected; screen shake is disabled when it is on.
- The palette is chosen to keep terrain, monsters and items distinguishable
  without relying on colour alone.

If something is not usable for you, please email us and describe it. Accessibility
reports get priority over feature requests.

## Known limitations

Underdeep is being actively developed. At the current version:

- The item, spell and town systems are incomplete.
- Only the dungeon is implemented; the town above it is not yet available.
- Some action-bar buttons report that their feature "is not yet written into the
  deep" rather than doing nothing silently.
